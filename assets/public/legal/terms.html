These are the rules for using __INSTANCE_NAME__. They are short on purpose.

1. No KYC

__INSTANCE_NAME__ does not do identity checks. We will never ask you for a government ID, a passport, a selfie or face scan, a phone number, or any proof of your real name or age in order to create an account, keep one, or unlock a feature. The date of birth you enter at sign-up is taken on trust and is never checked against a document.

If an account causes trouble, we act on what it does, not on who it belongs to. This section comes before every other rule on this page. If a law that applies to us ever forces a change to it, we will announce that before it takes effect.

2. Other instances

You are free to register on, and use, any other instance, at the same time as this one. We will never ban, restrict or punish anyone for having an account somewhere else, or for using another instance. Your accounts elsewhere are your business.

3. Your account

  • You are responsible for what happens on your account, so keep your password safe.
  • Do not impersonate other people or pretend to be staff.
  • Do not sell, trade or give away access to someone else's account.

4. How to behave

  • Follow the community guidelines.
  • No harassment, threats or hate against people.
  • No spam, scams or attempts to take over accounts.
  • No illegal content, and nothing that exploits or endangers children.
  • Do not attack or overload the service.

5. Moderation and appeals

Staff can add a violation to an account when these rules are broken. Violations count against your account standing until they expire, and may come with actions such as a warning, limited access, a quarantine, a temporary ban or a ban. You can see every violation on your Account Standing page and appeal any of them. Staff review each appeal, and if it is accepted the violation and what it did are undone.

6. Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you let __INSTANCE_NAME__ store it and show it to the people you send it to, which is what a chat service has to do to work.

7. Changes

We may update these terms. When we do, we will say so in an announcement. Using the service after that means you accept the new version. Questions can go to the contact address below, if one is set.
